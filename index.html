AUG 21, 2026
SERVER REBRANDING & NEW CHAPTER

@everyone We are excited to announce a massive update for our community! Romich Production is officially evolving into MEGADETH – ROBLOX.

🎸 From this moment on, we are focusing all our energy, efforts, and passion exclusively on building the ultimate Megadeth virtual experience in Roblox!

What’s Changing?
• Full Focus on Megadeth: Every upcoming concert, virtual venue, stage design, and event will be entirely dedicated to bringing the iconic energy of Megadeth to life.
• Upgraded Shows: Expect higher quality audio setups, custom Roblox animations, detailed light shows, and immersive concert arenas.
• Community Hub: Our server is now the primary headquarters for all thrash metal fans and Roblox builders supporting our show productions.

Thank you all for being part of this journey. Get ready for the heaviest virtual shows yet — stay tuned for venue previews and tour dates!

Tonight in Lisbon, the final chapter of this incredible European journey was written.

What a ride it has been.

We witnessed sold-out crowds, incredible fan projects, thousands of voices singing together, and an atmosphere that words can barely describe. The energy you brought to every single show fueled this tour from beginning to end.

To everyone who joined us somewhere along the road — thank you. You made BREAKOUT: HIBERNATION OF THE NATIONS more than just a tour. You made it an experience we will never forget.

Europe, our time together in 2026 has come to a close.
